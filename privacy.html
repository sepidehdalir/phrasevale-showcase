Updated October 18, 2026. Phrasevale is currently in development. This page describes the implemented local workflow; it does not imply App Store submission or approval.

Your audio stays under your control

Phrasevale copies audio you explicitly select through Files into the app's local storage. Source audio, phrase positions, collections, presets and recorded responses are processed on your device. The developer does not receive this content. No account, external AI service, advertising or tracking SDK is used.

Microphone

Microphone permission is requested when you choose Record response. You can decline and keep listening to sources. You can change microphone permission in iOS Settings. Phrasevale does not use speech recognition or score your voice.

Backups and deletion

Private app media is excluded from automatic device backup. An explicit Export practice backup action creates a portable file containing original audio, saved phrase positions and responses. A confirmation explains this before you choose a destination. Files providers or destinations you choose may store or sync content under their own settings. Anyone with the exported backup can access its audio.

Removing a source deletes its app copy and paired responses after the updated library is saved. It does not delete the original in Files or previously exported backups. Uninstalling removes the app sandbox. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your practice. Temporary export files are removed when the share sheet dismisses.

Purchases and local diagnostics

Apple processes purchases and restoration through StoreKit. Phrasevale reads verified entitlements on device; the developer operates no payment server and receives no card information through the app. Content-free event counts are stored locally for app reliability and funnel diagnostics. They are not transmitted to the developer. Audio, transcripts, source names and paths are not logged as analytics.

Contact and website hosting

If you email support, the information you choose to include is received for responding to your request. Do not send private audio unless you intend to share it. This project website is hosted by GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process standard web request information according to its own policies. Those website requests are distinct from local practice data in the app.
