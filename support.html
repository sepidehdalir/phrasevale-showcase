Support

Phrasevale is in development and is not yet available on the App Store. For support, contact hello@arvinify.com.

Import your own audio

Use playable, unprotected WAV, AIFF, M4A, MP3 or CAF files, up to 64 MB and 30 minutes, mono or stereo, 8–96 kHz. If a Files provider cannot open a source, download it in Files first and try again. Streaming links, protected media and video imports are unsupported.

Record and compare

Save a phrase between 0.2 and 60 seconds, allow microphone access when asked, then record your response. If access is denied, enable Phrasevale's microphone permission in iOS Settings. Compare by ear; the app does not transcribe or evaluate your speech.

Protect your practice

Private audio is excluded from automatic system backup. Export a practice backup before uninstalling or replacing the library. Backups include original audio and responses; keep them private. Restore validates a backup before replacing current data. Invalid input leaves the current library intact.

Lifetime Pro

The free source includes the full practice loop. The one-time upgrade adds multiple sources, named collections and reusable presets. Use Restore Purchases in settings or the upgrade sheet with the Apple account used for purchase. Previously saved sources remain accessible after an entitlement is revoked.

Storage limits: 20 sources, 500 phrases, 160 MB audio per library, 224 MB portable backup. Recordings are limited to 60 seconds. Prices are supplied by the App Store; no subscription.
